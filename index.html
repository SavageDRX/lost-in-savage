How to play

  1. You're standing somewhere on a Savage map. Drag the view to look all the way around, and scroll or use + / − to zoom.
  2. The compass at the top shows which way you're facing. North is the top of every minimap.
  3. Pick the map you think it is, then click its minimap where you think you're standing.
  4. The right map scores up to 5,000 points; the closer your pin, the more you get. The wrong map scores nothing.

Look for mines, the command centres, bridges, cliffs, snow and tree types. They all match the real map.
